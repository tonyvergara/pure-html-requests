What's It All About?

Body & Soul Jazz Weekend is where music meets movement, art meets community, and joy meets expression. Over three sun-soaked days, immerse yourself in the vibrant world of jazz through live performances, interactive workshops, and artistic showcases that celebrate the genre's rich history and boundless creativity.

Whether you're a longtime jazz enthusiast or just discovering its magic, this festival welcomes everyone to dance, learn, create, and connect in a space designed for pure celebration of culture and art.

Good to Know

The festival takes place at River Park Arts District, located at 450 Riverside Boulevard. The venue is easily accessible by public transit (Green Line to Arts District stop) and has ample parking available for $10/day. The entire festival grounds are wheelchair accessible.

Friday: 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM
Saturday: 12:00 PM - 11:00 PM
Sunday: 12:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Gates open one hour before official start times. Some workshops require pre-registration, so arrive early!

Bring comfortable shoes for dancing, sunscreen, a reusable water bottle (free refill stations available), and cash or card for food vendors. Blankets and low-back chairs are welcome for outdoor seating areas. Please leave large bags and coolers at home.

Absolutely! We have a dedicated Kids' Jazz Corner with age-appropriate workshops, instrument petting zoo, and performances. Children under 12 enter free with an adult. Stroller parking and family rest areas are available throughout the grounds.

Service animals are always welcome. Unfortunately, due to crowd sizes and safety concerns, we cannot accommodate pets. We recommend leaving your furry friends at home or with a trusted sitter.

We'd love your involvement! Visit our website to sign up as a festival volunteer (receive a free t-shirt and meal vouchers). Musicians interested in performing at jam sessions can register on-site at the Artist Lounge. Applications for next year's lineup open in January.
