Body & Soul Jazz Weekend

Three days of music, movement, and creative expression

When: September 15–17, 2026
Where: River Park Arts District
Access: All ages welcome

What to Expect

Body & Soul Jazz Weekend brings together live performances, interactive workshops, and artistic showcases celebrating jazz's rich history and ongoing evolution. Over three days, experience the genre through music, dance, visual art, and community.

Whether you're a longtime jazz enthusiast or new to the art form, the festival offers programming for all levels. Come to watch, participate, learn, or simply enjoy the atmosphere.

Weekend Schedule

Opening Night Concert

Friday 7:00 PM

Start the weekend with performances by internationally acclaimed jazz ensembles. Outdoor main stage with covered seating.

Movement & Dance Workshops

Saturday 2:00 PM

Learn swing, blues, and contemporary jazz dance from experienced instructors. Beginner through advanced sessions available.

Visual Arts Showcase

Saturday 4:00 PM

Browse jazz-inspired paintings and sculptures in the gallery space. Watch artists create live murals throughout the afternoon.

Open Jam Sessions

Saturday 8:00 PM

Musicians of all skill levels welcome to join impromptu jam sessions. Instruments and equipment provided.

Food & Culture Village

All weekend

Local food vendors and artisan market open throughout festival hours. International cuisines and jazz-era specialties available.

Closing Performance

Sunday 6:00 PM

Festival finale featuring collaborative performance with participating artists. Main stage, general admission.

Practical Information

The festival is at River Park Arts District, 450 Riverside Boulevard. Take the Green Line to Arts District station (5-minute walk) or park on-site for $10 per day. The venue is fully wheelchair accessible.

Friday: 6:00 PM – 11:00 PM
Saturday: 12:00 PM – 11:00 PM
Sunday: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Gates open one hour before official start times. Some workshops require advance registration.

Bring comfortable shoes for dancing, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle (free refill stations on-site). Blankets and low-back chairs are allowed in outdoor seating areas. Large bags and outside food are not permitted.

Yes. We have a dedicated Kids' Jazz Corner with age-appropriate workshops and activities. Children under 12 enter free with an adult. Family rest areas and stroller parking available throughout the grounds.

Service animals are always welcome. Pets cannot be accommodated due to crowd safety and comfort concerns.

Volunteer opportunities are available on our website (volunteers receive a t-shirt and meal vouchers). Musicians interested in jam sessions can register at the Artist Lounge on-site. Applications for next year's performance lineup open in January.
